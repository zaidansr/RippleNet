# Real-data feasibility decision

Decision date: 6 November 2026. This is a bounded feasibility review, not a real-market forecasting result. Three subagents examined official sources, the information/target mathematics, and a small sample's actual schema. The integrated decision below is the research judgement drawn from those audits.

## Decision and scope

**Do not claim real-market validation of the proposed observation-error versus genuine-shock mechanism, or expand the model architecture on the present evidence.** None of the reviewed candidates yet establishes the required combination of auditable outcomes, admissible as-of candidate inputs, and a verified basis for the intended data use and publication. This does not establish that suitable data do not exist elsewhere.

**An observable-target replay prototype is a separate, conditional engineering step.** It may proceed once the selected sample's intended use is supported and its original sequence or prefix-causal reconstruction is verified. Its success would establish data plumbing, not the original mechanism or financial usefulness. No real-data model was fitted in this audit.

The previous synthetic conclusions remain unchanged. On the frozen observation-noise grid, retention gains were small and all adjusted upper bounds were below 0.23%, against a 5% practical reference. These findings apply to that known-time, known-parameter, isolated-event task. They neither prove market-wide impossibility nor supply evidence that a larger architecture will help. See the [complete sensitivity report](noise-sensitivity-results.md).

## Why the original claim cannot yet be tested

The simulator supplies a true latent state and an exhaustive intervention category. Ordinary market records do not automatically supply either. A cancelled execution may have occurred and affected subsequent trading. An uncorrected execution is not thereby a verified genuine shock. Mixed causes and unresolved cases must remain possible.

A later match-linked correction can answer a narrower question: will this record receive a qualifying correction within a fixed window? That is a legitimate supervised task even though the label arrives later. At each historical fit cutoff, however, only then-matured outcomes may enter training. Test labels must receive the same declared maturation opportunity or be treated as censored.

Equally, a future observed midpoint return can be a valid forecasting target without being a measured latent efficient return or an executable profit. The source, eligibility rules and horizon determine what conclusion an experiment can support. A useful correction classifier need not improve this future-price target.

The inspected reconstructed book does not close these identification gaps. Comparing two transformations of the same underlying feed also does not create independent measurements of truth. The [mathematical audit](observable-target-audit.md) derives why auxiliary information must change the conditional target mean to reduce optimal squared forecasting error.

## Required contract for a bounded replay

Before interpreting another forecast comparison, record the following in a frozen specification:

1. **Target and scope:** one venue/instrument, fixed consecutive development interval, a deterministic eligible reference price, and one justified horizon. Name an ordinal horizon as exchange-message count or recorded-row count as appropriate. Do not translate either into client latency without evidence. Define missing, stale, halted and invalid-price handling before looking at target outcomes.
2. **As-of information:** immutable source records and revisions, stable IDs/order, and the declared availability convention. A snapshot must depend only on its allowed prefix. Inserting or changing later records must leave earlier features and origin selection unchanged. Multi-source alignment needs its own availability evidence.
3. **Outcome evidence:** for a correction task, retain original/revision linkage, qualifying action semantics, maturation window and censoring. Count actually linked mature cases before attempting statistical claims. Normal order cancellations and price outliers must not be substituted for independently verified recording errors. Keep unverified and mixed cases distinct.
4. **Admissible features:** list each candidate signal and show when it becomes available. A feature is not required to be predictive to pass this audit; its usefulness is what the subsequent experiment tests. Supply the same full information to all architecture comparisons.
5. **Use and provenance:** establish the basis for local research and separately for any public data or derived outputs. Retain source URL, retrieval date, hash, schema/version and bounded acquisition limits. Keep restricted material outside Git. Spending still requires explicit owner approval.

These are data-readiness checks, not requirements for the user to provide facts that can be investigated. The first replay should be a small schema/causality check. Its duration and event count do not automatically establish statistical power or generalisation.

## Next experiment that distinguishes competing explanations

If the contract is satisfied and a timely quality signal exists, freeze a chronological information ablation before opening a protected final period. Use the observed target, not unobserved latent returns. Start with credible simple predictors: zero-return persistence and a regularised linear autoregression. Use development data to select a small justified baseline set and train only on then-available features and matured labels.

| Arm | Allowed inputs | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A: primary baseline | Primary as-of history | What can the primary stream predict? |
| B: full-information baseline | The same history plus the audited timely signal | Does the additional information help an ordinary predictor? |
| C: candidate, only if B warrants it | Exactly B's inputs | Does the proposed mechanism add value beyond the information itself? |

This separates competing explanations efficiently. A reliable, meaningful B-over-A gain together with an interval excluding a useful C-over-B gain supports information value without demonstrated additional architecture value. Better correction classification without forecast improvement supports a data-quality use claim only. A C-over-B gain would justify further model investigation; it would not alone prove a causal error-versus-shock interpretation. If intervals exclude the prespecified useful gain for the tested predictors, stop this forecasting branch for that target and horizon. Wide intervals remain inconclusive; lack of significance is not evidence of no useful effect.

Making C conditional on B is a resource gate, not a mathematical assertion that B exhausts the information in its inputs. An inadequate linear predictor can miss a nonlinear signal that another model could exploit. Skipping C after an unpromising B therefore does not establish that every architecture must fail. Any decision to investigate that possibility should rest on a specific, independently motivated hypothesis rather than repeated tuning against the protected final period.

Fix the smallest meaningful effect for the new target using its intended purpose before final evaluation; the synthetic 5% reference is not automatically transferable. Estimate sample requirements from development event frequency and dependent loss variability. Protect the final chronological block, account for overlapping targets and serial dependence, and prespecify primary contrasts and multiplicity. Report effect sizes, uncertainty, coverage and failures, including null results. A tiny schema sample is not a final test, and a single venue/period does not demonstrate broad generalisation.

No timely signal or defensible mechanism labels have been established here. Consequently the immediate next research operation is the contract's bounded evidence/linkage check, conditional on a suitable data-use basis. A broader search, a different target, or future-correction prediction must be declared as a new research question rather than presented as confirmation of the original one.
