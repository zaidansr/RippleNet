# Public financial-data audit: evidence, timing and observable targets

This source-review subtask checked official provider documentation and public directory listings on 6 November 2026 without downloading archives, creating accounts, purchasing services or performing external writes. Separately, the engineering agent downloaded a bounded official legacy LOBSTER ZIP into private working storage and inspected its schema; see [data-engineering feasibility](data-engineering-feasibility.md). This document reproduces no sample rows or market values.

## Finding

No reviewed option yet establishes a free, ready-to-use dataset with defensible, independently auditable two-class observation-error versus genuine-market-shock outcomes together with contemporaneously available candidate inputs supporting that distinction. Two exchange formats contain meaningful subsequent correction evidence; the small reconstructed-book alternative omits that evidence. These are three realistic acquisition candidates, not three validated RippleNet datasets.

Supervised outcome labels need not be known at the forecast origin. A later broken-trade or cancellation message can legitimately define a future-correction target, with a declared label-maturation window. The restriction concerns prediction inputs: that message cannot be supplied before it arrives. Training at a historical cutoff must also respect which outcome labels had matured by that cutoff. Predictive usefulness of an admissible candidate feature set remains an empirical question, not a requirement that this data audit prove an advantage in advance.

An exchange cancellation is evidence about an exchange's treatment of a transaction. It does not directly reveal a latent efficient price or prove that the original published bytes were misrecorded. A transaction may have genuinely occurred and subsequently been invalidated. Conversely, absence of a correction does not prove a true economic shock. Those distinctions are the audit's interpretation, rather than labels supplied by the providers.

## 1. Nasdaq official Historical TotalView-ITCH samples

**Access and scale.** The [official ITCH FAQ](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/Content/TechnicalSupport/FAQs/ITCH_FAQ.pdf) links the unauthenticated [Nasdaq sample directory](https://emi.nasdaq.com/ITCH/Nasdaq%20ITCH/). Its listing contains gzip files: for example `12302019.NASDAQ_ITCH50.gz` is approximately 3.52 GB and `01302020.NASDAQ_ITCH50.gz` approximately 5.60 GB compressed. A 58.9 MB ZIP also exists, but its schema, date and usefulness were not inspected. No archive was downloaded. Public listing does not establish an open redistribution licence.

**Format and evidence.** [ITCH 5.0 specification](https://nasdaqtrader.com/content/technicalsupport/specifications/dataproducts/NQTVITCHSpecification.pdf), data types and §1.5.3: sequenced binary messages carry nanoseconds-since-midnight timestamps. Type `B` links a broken execution to its prior match number. The break may follow a clearly erroneous execution; the break is final and does not change the current book. Order cancel/delete messages `X`/`D` are different events. These protocol facts do not establish that any selected sample contains a useful number of breaks.

**Point-in-time interpretation.** The earlier trade's later break is valid future outcome evidence for a correction-prediction study. It can enter the predictor as a feature only after its own feed position/time; backdating it to the execution leaks future information. Feed timestamps are not a verified local subscriber arrival clock. Other candidate targets are a declared Nasdaq-observed future midpoint or transaction statistic, not latent returns. Match completeness, break counts, latency and sample-specific research/publication rights remain unverified.

**Disposition:** strongest raw schema for a cancellation-evidence feasibility audit, but current visible files are substantial and rights/event coverage remain unresolved.

## 2. NYSE official TAQ Integrated/Trades samples

**Access and scale.** NYSE's [historical product page](https://www.nyse.com/market-data/historical/taq-integrated-feed) links a public sample directory. The [Integrated listing](https://ftp.nyse.com/Historical%20Data%20Samples/TAQ%20NYSE%20INTEGRATED%20FEED/) currently shows July 2026 channel gzip files, approximately 238 MB–1.39 GB each. A smaller [National Trades listing](https://ftp.nyse.com/Historical%20Data%20Samples/TAQ%20NYSE%20NATIONAL%20TRADES/) contains October 2023 files around 14.2–14.7 MB; those are trades, not a complete book. Listings were inspected without downloading archives. The main [historical overview](https://www.nyse.com/market-data/historical) describes end-of-day or T+1 delivery; historical distribution delay differs from the original feed-event chronology.

**Format and evidence.** [TAQ products specification v2.4a](https://www.nyse.com/publicdocs/nyse/data/TAQ_XDP_Products_Client_Spec_v2.4a.pdf), §§2,14,15,24: flat-file fields include message sequence, source time at nanosecond granularity, symbol sequence and identifiers. Cancellation `112` is for Integrated; `221` is for Trades. `TradeID` identifies the earlier execution being cancelled. Cross correction `113` and trade correction `222` are distinct. The specification says a trade-cancel message is issued when an earlier report was erroneous and affects past trades. The correction/cancellation event, not the original execution time, determines when this evidence becomes usable. Sample event counts and matching success have not been measured.

**Rights matter here.** The official [Sample Data Agreement](https://www.nyse.com/publicdocs/nyse/data/NYSE_Sample_Data_Agreement.pdf), linked from NYSE's [policies/contracts page](https://www.nyse.com/market-data/pricing-policies-contracts-guidelines), describes no-charge internal use to assess subscribing, excludes other productive use, restricts onward supply/copies, and defines sample services to include derived analyses/calculations. This is not an open research or public-GitHub grant. Applicability and any separate permission for a particular FTP sample require verification; no agreement was accepted in this audit. Do not publish raw rows or derived sample results on the assumption that public download availability supplies those rights.

**Disposition:** relevant error-report evidence, but neither research/publication permission nor a sufficiently labelled free sample is established. A venue midpoint could be an observable target if the needed book stream and permissions exist; corrected trade records still do not measure latent economic truth.

## 3. LOBSTER legacy samples derived from Nasdaq ITCH

**Access and format.** The [legacy sample page](https://php.lobsterdata.com/info/DataSamples.php) exposes small downloads, including GOOG level 1 listed at 0.5 MB and AMZN level 1 at 0.7 MB. These are derived from the official Nasdaq sample, not an independent venue reference. The [output documentation](https://php.lobsterdata.com/info/DataStructure.php) describes paired CSV message/book rows, timestamps in seconds after midnight with precision dependent on period, order identifiers, event types, prices scaled by 10,000, and book levels. Type 2 cancels a resting order; it does not label an erroneous execution. A row's message produces that row's updated book, which matters when defining prediction timing.

**Missing evidence.** The provider's [reconstruction details, item 4](https://php.lobsterdata.com/info/help_reconstruction.php) explicitly says broken-trade messages are ignored in reconstruction. Its [general FAQ](https://php.lobsterdata.com/info/help_faq_general.php) also distinguishes the historical book shown to traders from later cancellations. Therefore this convenient format cannot by itself supply the raw broken-trade audit label or independent ground truth. Crossed quotes, sparse levels, or large returns must not be promoted to verified recording-error labels.

**Rights and access unknowns.** The [legacy terms page](https://php.lobsterdata.com/termsAndConditions.php) says academic subscription terms are supplied with the contract and provides no standalone public terms PDF. That does not establish unrestricted research or redistribution rights for legacy samples. The [current main site](https://lobsterdata.com/) advertises approval-based access and a separate book-owner sample offer; do not infer that this gated offer grants rights for legacy links. No sample-data publication right was verified.

**Disposition:** small enough for a bounded schema/observable-midpoint prototype if applicable rights are confirmed, but unsuitable for validating the proposed error-versus-shock mechanism on its own.

## Required information contract before another forecasting study

1. **Observable target:** fix venue, instruments, session, horizon and an as-published reference such as the first valid venue midpoint at or after a declared future time. Define stale/missing quote and correction handling before inspecting outcomes. Describe it as a reference-source forecast target, not a measured latent efficient return.
2. **Evidence clock:** preserve original message order and separate execution time, correction-generation time, feed dissemination time and local reception time where known. Future corrections may be labels for supervised development/evaluation; their late arrival does not disqualify prospective prediction. They cannot be historical prediction inputs before arrival, and a training fold may use only labels available by its fit cutoff. No inspected archive establishes a subscriber's exact historical reception clock.
3. **Label definition:** use a narrow exchange-action label such as subsequently cancelled execution, with identifier linkage, a declared maturation window and right-censoring policy. Do not label every uncancelled record a genuine shock. Official status/halt events support only their stated administrative meaning; they do not establish an exclusive latent shock category.
4. **Independent reference:** two formats derived from the same ITCH file are not two independent measurements. Cross-venue disagreements can reflect genuine market differences, clocks and trading rules. A reliable contemporaneous reference and any data-quality signal must be identified, not assumed.
5. **Acquisition stop rule:** confirm terms, bounded file size, usable identifiers, actual correction counts and timing before downloading substantial archives or running a forecast comparison. Preserve code, synthetic fixtures and source links publicly; adding third-party data or derived results needs verified rights for that specific material.

The efficient next step is a small, rights-cleared message-linkage and evidence-latency audit. Establish auditable outcome labels, an observable target and an admissible as-of candidate feature set before testing predictive usefulness. If those foundations cannot be established, stop the present financial forecasting-improvement branch rather than infer labels from price outliers or inject synthetic corruption and call it empirical validation. A narrowly relabelled future-correction prediction study may still be legitimate when its outcomes arrive later; it would not establish the original error-versus-genuine-shock claim. No reviewed provider supplies the missing latent-state ground truth.
