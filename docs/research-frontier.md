## Research directions requiring an explicit new question

1. **Conditional synthetic information experiment.** Ask whether a genuinely independent second measurement would create useful next-return information under a newly declared generative model. Use exact Bayes risk before developing another architecture, a duplicate-channel negative control, a shared-disturbance control and comparisons using the same inputs. Stipulating that errors affect only one channel while state shocks affect both builds distinguishing information into the experiment. Any positive result would demonstrate a conditional information effect, not identify real market causes, establish novelty or validate an available market sensor. Do not select sensor quality after observing gains or reinterpret the earlier null results as success.
2. **Retrieved-file forecasting question.** Explicitly change the estimand to predicting prices recorded in the downloaded historical file. Freeze the target, horizon, chronology, baselines, dependent-loss inference and practical margin before evaluating protected outcomes. The tiny inspected day is a plumbing sample, not an established training/test corpus. A result would retain the archive-version and availability limitations and would not test the original cause mechanism.
3. **Bounded external evidence search.** Define a precise acquisition target and stop rule for original values/order, revision linkage and defensible outcomes. Finding a correction ledger alone still does not identify a pure recording error or prove every uncorrected event a genuine shock. No new acquisition, provider contact or paid resource was used in this review. Spending continues to require explicit owner approval.

These directions answer different questions. The owner has been asked which to pursue. This review does not choose a new research claim or launch a new experiment by default. Until that choice is resolved, the current archive's original-mechanism evidence gate remains unmet.
