# Scientific review of the synthesis manuscript

Reviewed `research-paper.md` and `conclusion.md` against finalized stage reports and earlier independent reproductions. This is an internal AI-assisted audit within the same research workflow, not external peer review. No experiment, model, target, dataset or statistical setting was added or rerun for the manuscript.

## Central finding

The conclusion is supported at its stated scope: the work does not validate a RippleNet financial forecasting architecture, establish real observation-error versus shock identification, or demonstrate profitability. The manuscript retains the negative results, metric disagreements, coverage restrictions and differences in information sets needed to assess that conclusion. It avoids turning failure of these tested claims into a universal impossibility result.

The numerical summaries checked against the stage records agree. In particular:

- V1 fails its practical gate and deteriorates under price-derived corruption. Its category tradeoff and lack of reliable advantage over pooled noise are not described as causal identification.
- V2's retained-versus-projection estimate, adjusted interval, frozen-weight benefit and type-log-score improvement are correctly separated. Known intervention timing, known parameters and absence of a clean-event population are disclosed.
- Conditional-risk reconstruction is correctly identified as reuse of disclosed histories. The 95.763% innovation share, 0.03564% retention benefit and 4.27116% perfect-current-state ceiling have the correct references and model-conditional interpretation. They are not samplewise realised-loss bounds.
- All five noise cells are reported without choosing a winner. The auxiliary diagnostic separates the 68.6824% information reduction from the differently normalized 1.80895% nonlinear reduction; 99.16% refers to captured absolute benefit. Quadrature agreement is numerical, not statistical replication.
- Both 2017 studies preserve their MSE improvements and worse MAE. The fresh delayed target retains improvement, and attenuation is not claimed to prove bid/ask bounce. Bootstrap dependence assumptions and noncircular endpoint weighting are disclosed.
- The Kraken pilot preserves the mechanical-baseline null, 0.7606% midpoint MSE improvement, worse MAE, three-session restriction and 17.73% grid scoring coverage. Its daily percentages are not averaged to manufacture the primary risk ratio.

## Mathematical and inferential checks

The conditional-risk decomposition, nested-information identity and trade/midpoint gap identity are consistent with the stated assumptions. Target indices, trade-count versus clock-time horizons, shared inputs and target-specific fitting are distinguished. Neither later-arriving target validity nor a future delayed-start price is presented as an available origin feature. The restricted paired scoring population is explicit.

No unsupported significance, external replication, novel-theorem or architecture-superiority claim was identified. The test-suite count is expressly implementation evidence. Source hashes are not misrepresented as proof of original market availability. The real-market studies remain observations of retrieved records rather than operational execution tests or independently adjudicated cause labels.

## Pre-publication corrections requested

1. Change “V1 fits a small moment-matched mixture candidate” to “V1 evaluates …”. The fitted OLS component and fixed mixture settings should not be conflated with training a new architecture.
2. Replace “It is not a reliable financial edge” with “It does not establish a reliable financial edge.” The evidence limits a claim; it does not prove that every possible edge is absent.
3. Make the Binance-derived material's attribution, adaptation/change notice and applicable CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license link explicit. Keep that scope distinct from Tardis-derived aggregate research; do not imply a blanket manuscript/data license. Preserve all restrictions on vendor observations and trained artifacts.

No Tardis learned coefficients, selected regularization values, fitted scalers/intercepts/medians, event rows or per-origin predictions were found in the reviewed draft. The final rendered PDF still needs a separate visual/export check; this review does not certify its layout or external-submission readiness.

## Resolution

All three requested corrections were incorporated. A final read-only subagent check confirmed them and found no remaining scientific blocker. The completed pilot's final report has been internally reviewed and has not been externally peer reviewed.
